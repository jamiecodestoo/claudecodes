Questions about
applying to 8x.

How the application, the assignment, portfolios and referrals work.

What happens after I apply?

For roles hired through this platform, submitting your application issues the role’s practical assignment. Open the brief, use any attached source files, and submit your work from your candidate account. Some platform roles have a separate application site.

What does the application include?

The application includes shared questions, including your last salary, and questions specific to the role. Read the role’s requirements before starting. The practical assignment is issued on submission.

What happens when the assignment timer reaches zero?

You can still submit. The countdown is tracked and never enforced, so nothing locks when it reaches zero. The assignment brief states the expected time.

How do I submit the practical work?

Your submission can include files, links, written context, and a walkthrough URL. Follow the brief and make your own contribution clear.

Will my application or assignment become public?

Your applications and assignment submissions stay private. A public portfolio is separate: only the content you choose to publish appears there.

Can I make a portfolio without applying?

Yes. Anyone with a verified account can create a portfolio. It is free to create and share, and no application is required.

Can I edit or unpublish my portfolio?

Yes. Edit privately and publish your changes when ready. You can unpublish while keeping your draft. Your chosen address stays fixed. Copies already saved elsewhere may remain.

How does the referral reward work?

If someone applies through your link, is hired, and starts, 8x finance pays you 10 percent of their first-month salary. Your referral view shows the role, status, and date, never the applicant’s name or email.

## 1. Company facts
- [V] **Legal entity and funding:** 8x Social, Inc. is a Delaware company. SEC Form D, filed 2026-08-20, shows $5,557,496 in equity sold out of $7,057,496 offered, from 20 accredited investors under Rule 506(b). Jaka Bavdek and Theo Bui are the only officers and directors; revenue "Decline to Disclose". https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2151226/000215122626000001/primary_doc.xml
- [V] **Pre-seed:** "$125K SAFE for 8% fixed ownership" from Entrepreneur First. They passed EF's Investment Committee on 12 Jan 2026. https://jakabavdek.com/blog/entrepreneur-first.html
- [V] **Timeline:**
  - The founders met at EF London in autumn 2025 and went through four ideas in about two months.
  - "€250K ARR in five weeks", "80% close rate", "17% cold outreach response rate".
  - Moved to SF in late January 2026.
  - About $500K ARR with 2,000+ active creators in Feb 2026. https://jakabavdek.com/blog/san-francisco.html
- [V] **Team size:** "core team of six or seven people … Everything past that core is outcome-based and on demand: you don't hire the team, you buy the result" (June 2026). https://jakabavdek.com/blog/billion-human-company.html. LinkedIn: 48 employees, size 11–50, 10,224 followers. https://www.linkedin.com/company/8x-human/
- [V] **How 8x.careers/about describes the company:** "human orchestration company", "Remote-first, built from San Francisco". Organised in verticals, each with a vertical lead and "one key metric". The aim is "billions of people under management".
- **Not found:** YC affiliation, named seed investors, Glassdoor, mainstream press.
- **Misattribution warning:** a €5.7M round from VentureFriends, Portfolion and Götze belongs to Kodesage, not 8x.

## 2. Sister sites
- **8x.social**
  - **What it is:** a managed creator network posting organic UGC. Self-serve has a 7-day trial; Full Service costs at least $5k/month. Creators get base pay per post plus an uncapped view bonus.
  - **Claims:** "250k+ creators". Views are given as 300M+, 5.3B+ and 100M+; countries as 140+, 60+, 61 and "over 50" on different pages.
  - **Tone:** loud Title Case hype. Has a "more than countries" bug.
- **8x.business (8x LinkedIn)**
  - **What it is:** a B2B LinkedIn creator marketplace with escrowed per-post pricing ($150–$525 shown).
  - **Claims:** "270K+ creators profiled". Benchmarks are labelled "illustrative … not an audited study".
  - **Tone:** calm and plain.
- **8xresearch.com:** AI-moderated user interviews. Free, $99/month or custom. Participants are paid $3–10 per session. Tone: soft, British spelling.
- **8x.sale**
  - **What it is:** remote cold-calling reps, no experience needed.
  - **Pay:** US $8.75/hr plus $20 and $70 meeting bonuses.
  - **Tone:** very plain and compliance-careful.
- [V] **8x Karma:** a Reddit growth engine. It went from a 4:23 AM Slack idea on 4 Sept to launch on 21 Sept, **17 days**. *8x.life does not list it.* https://www.linkedin.com/posts/activity-7507911854627786752-gEIF

## 3. Founders [V]
- **Jaka Bavdek, CEO**
  - **Background:** small-town Slovenia. Sold love padlocks at 15. Ran exam lectures for 300+ students at 18. e-Matura grew to "Slovenia's #1", 100k+ students a year, in 11 markets. LBS Masters.
  - **Beliefs:**
    - "Every CEO should be shipping code."
    - "Global arbitrage is the real unlock. We have 20-year-olds from Pakistan managing six-figure contracts."
    - "Speed and systems aren't opposites."
  - **On pace:**
    - Berlin: "Most people leave at 6pm."
    - SF: "We're in the office practically 24/7."
  - **Mission:** opening a path from small countries to SF.
- **Theo Bui, CTO**
  - French-American. MEng Design Engineering, Imperial. Previously an ML engineer at Cleo ("youngest hire out of university"), then co-founder and CTO of Trippl.
  - Built the 8x platform himself.
  - In his words: "Coordinating 5 humans already breaks most teams. Now imagine 1,000." "no interview needed when you can ship fast."
  - Jaka sums up the split as "He builds. I distribute."

## 4. Culture evidence [V]
- **8x hiring post:** "Fully remote, no CV needed for most roles. Show us what you can do." Market Lead internships run as a "2-week trial, for students".
- **Paid-ads post:** "4 products. 10 markets. 5 channels. one person. … kill what doesn't work in days, not months."
- **Hired from work, not interviews:** an engineer was hired "without a single interview" on the strength of hackathon wins. A merch design competition: "winner gets hired".
- **Assignment-first goes back to e-Matura:** country managers were hired via "a real assignment".
- **Leverage:** one account manager went from 15 to 50+ creator accounts in 5 months. The team built its own meeting platform, CRM and outreach tools.
- **Team members visible publicly:** in India, Pakistan, Turkey and Slovenia.
- **Sceptical public comment:** "'no CV needed' correct, you'll need a client list instead."
- **Verified directly on 8x.careers (by me, not this agent):**
  - The application always asks for your last salary.
  - "Small budgets on purpose" (Paid Ads Manager listing).
  - "The timer is tracked and never enforced."
- **Not found publicly:** staff pay, equity, benefits, hours.

## 5. Contradictions with 8x.life's "250k+ humans managed, 50+ countries"
- **Agree:** 8x.social, the LinkedIn tagline and hiring post, 8x.careers/about.
- **Contradict, from primary sources:**
  - Jaka's homepage: "120,000+ humans … 30+ countries".
  - Jaka, June 2026: "121,000 people on the platform".
  - Theo's site: "50K+ humans … 30+ countries".
  - 8x.social's own regional breakdown adds up to about 10k.
  - Feb 2026: "2,000+ active creators".
- [INF] "250k" probably counts every sign-up or profiled creator. "Managed" overstates it.

## 6. Who would love or hate working here [INF]
- **Love:** hungry, credential-light builders, often outside the US, who can show work. Students who want to launch a market. Generalists who want a whole product, market or channel. AI-native tool-builders. People driven by metrics.
- **Hate:**
  - People who want defined scope, mentorship or predictable hours.
  - People who want published pay or late-stage stability.
  - Narrow specialists.
  - People put off by hype numbers.
  - People uneasy with creator seeding on fresh accounts or Reddit.
  - People who need a clear line between core employee and on-demand worker.

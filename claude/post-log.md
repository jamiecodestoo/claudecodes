I shipped a whole iOS app without a design handoff.

No specs doc.
No redlines.
No "can you move this 4px?" thread.

Because the designer and the developer were the same person. Me.

Here's what changed in my workflow ↓

1. I sketch the flow, not the screens.
The flow is the design. Screens are just where it stops.

2. I design in the build.
Real data, real device, real thumb. A mockup never told me a button was too far to reach. [VERIFY: a specific moment like this from LastLook]

3. Figma is for thinking, not for approval.
If a frame only exists to be signed off, I skip it.
